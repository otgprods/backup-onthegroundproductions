The brief

Culture, not a campaign.

JOSEPHINE is a cultural IP studio. It builds lasting worlds with artists and creators, then brings them to brands through content, live experiences and media partnerships.

The studio needed a home that speaks to brands and artists at the same time, and that feels like the culture it makes rather than a sales deck.

Stage 01 · Design

A gallery, not a pitch deck.

The site opens on a collage hero: classical painting over torn street posters, with the name set large underneath. Black, cream and a single red carry through every page.

Collage works run between the sections, so scrolling feels like walking through an exhibition.

Interaction · The J

Move through the J.

The J from the wordmark is drawn as a field of dots. Move your mouse or finger through it and the dots part around you, then drift back into the letter.

On a phone you can play with it without the page scrolling away, and a quick flick up or down still scrolls. Try it below.

Stage 02 · The slate

Worlds, then a private room.

The slate shows the cultural worlds in development, two in full and the rest in a carousel. A short brand film plays with its own cabaret soundtrack.

A private client room lets partners request a link to see the full slate, so the best work stays behind the door until it’s ready.

Stage 03 · Build

Fast, and easy to find.

Hand-coded with no templates and hosted on Cloudflare, so images, film and music load quickly on any connection. SEO and AI search are built in from day one, so the studio is easy to find on Google and in AI assistants.
