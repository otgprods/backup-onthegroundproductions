The brief

A front door of its own.

Quantock Lettings is a family-run lettings and property management business in the Vale of Taunton. It needed a home of its own, for two very different visitors: tenants looking for somewhere to live, and landlords looking for an agent they can trust.

Stage 01 · Brand positioning & strategy

Fair, honest and local.

We positioned Quantock Lettings around three values, being fair, honest and local, in a deep teal and soft white that feels calm and established.

Every page says who’s behind it, and Neil, Cheryl and Hattie are on the site, so landlords and tenants know who they’ll be dealing with.

Stage 02 · Design

Two audiences, one clear route.

The homepage opens with a home search for tenants, and two clear routes for landlords: transfer your property, or ask for a free rental valuation.

Homes to rent, a tenant area for maintenance and guides, and the real team, all in the same deep teal and soft white.

Stage 03 · Build

Open fees, simple forms.

Hand-coded and hosted on Cloudflare. Every fee is on the page, with no hidden costs, and the transfer, valuation, maintenance and contact forms go straight to the team.

A floating “Book a valuation” button follows landlords down every page, and the lettings team has its own phone line.

Visit quantocklettings.co.uk

Stage 04 · New homes

Pollards Yard.

When Quantock Lettings took on seven brand-new homes at Pollards Yard in Bridgwater, we built a page of their own to let them: the two house types with CGIs and floor plans, a photo gallery of a finished home, the full specification, a highlighted site plan and a register-your-interest form.

It has its own FAQs and structured data so the development can be found in search, and a New homes card on the homepage leads straight to it.

Beyond the website

Email signatures and guides.

The brand carries on past the site. We designed a Landlord Guide and a Tenant Guide, plain-English booklets updated for the Renters’ Rights Act, so everything people receive looks and sounds like the same business.

Every email from the office now ends the same way. We designed an HTML email signature for the Quantock Lettings team, with the lettings line, the office address and a short list of useful links: book a free valuation, the landlord and tenant guides, and report maintenance. Landlords and tenants are always one click from what they need, in Outlook, Apple Mail or on a phone.
