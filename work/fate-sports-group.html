The brief

Ready for the next chapter.

Fate Sports Group was founded by Le Mans class winner Chris Buncombe and former racing driver turned agent Struan Moore. They represent professional drivers across endurance, GT and single-seater racing, and were building a second side to the business: connecting brands with motorsport.

Their first site launched the business. As Fate grew into brand partnerships, they wanted a site that tells the whole story, with the pace and polish of the sport itself.

Stage 01 · Strategy & positioning

Two doors, equal weight.

Before designing anything, we worked out what Fate is and who the site is for. We split the business into two clear doors: Driver Management and Brand Partnerships.

“Brand Partnerships” replaced “Commercial”, so brands can see straight away that there’s a place for them. It sits apart from the drivers Fate represents and carries equal weight on the site. “Driver management” became the name for the other side, so both halves of the business are clear from the first scroll.

“You’ve done a super job in understanding what we discussed and our vision for evolving our web presence. The site looks mega!”

Chris Buncombe, FATE Sports Group
Stage 02 · Design

Bronze, cream and black.

A cinematic, high-motion site in Fate’s own palette. It opens on a 3D version of the Fate logo, which then gives way to the hero film from the paddock.

Big, confident type sets the tone: athletes, brands, motorsport.

Stage 03 · Build

Fast, everywhere.

Hand-coded with no templates, hosted on Cloudflare and built to load quickly on any connection. Scrolling is smooth on desktop, and the services move in the same swipeable carousel as the athletes.

Separate enquiry forms for brand partnerships and driver management send each lead straight to the team. Fate are on a monthly care plan, so the site keeps up with the season.

Every athlete has a page of their own, so a search for a driver leads straight to Fate: Jamie Chadwick, Ricky Taylor, Tim Tramnitz, Charlie Eastwood, Katsumasa Chiyo, Jonny Edgar, Sean Gelael, Matías Zagazeta and Zoe Florescu.

Visit fatesportsgroup.com

Launch

New website, now live.

A launch reel for Fate’s social channels walks through the athletes, driver profiles and services, and matching email signatures mean every message from the team carries the new look.
