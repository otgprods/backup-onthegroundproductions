The brief

From TV to private commissions.

Matt Leigh leads the garden builds on ITV’s Love Your Garden with Alan Titchmarsh, and designs award-winning gardens of his own in Devon.

He needed a site that turns that recognition into private commissions and brand partnerships.

Stage 01 · Brand positioning

The person behind the projects.

We positioned Matt as the person behind the projects on your screen: a hands-on builder with a designer’s eye. That shaped everything, from the line “Gardens that change lives” to a site organised around three kinds of work: design and build, garden makeovers, and charity and sensory builds.

Brands get their own route in, so partnership enquiries don’t get lost among garden ones.

Stage 02 · Design

Quiet, so the gardens can speak.

An editorial serif, garden greens and warm cream, with Matt’s own photography doing the work. His 3D walkthrough films sit at the heart of the site, so clients can see their garden before a spade goes in.

Stage 03 · Build

Light, fast and easy to update.

Hand-coded with no templates and hosted on Cloudflare, with films that load quickly and a layout that works just as well on a phone in the garden as on a laptop.
