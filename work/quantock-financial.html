Quantock Financial

A calm, trustworthy home for a Taunton mortgage and protection adviser.

The brief

Calm, clear, one tap from an adviser.

Quantock Financial is a mortgage and protection adviser in Taunton, Somerset. Money decisions matter, so the site needed to feel calm and trustworthy, and make it easy to talk to an adviser.

Stage 01 · Brand positioning

Care, not products.

We positioned Quantock around care rather than products: “Taking care of your financial goals, so you can focus on what matters most.”

The real advisers and their Google rating sit at the very top, so you can see who you’ll be speaking to before you scroll.

Stage 02 · Design

Deep teal and soft white.

Warm family photography, a clear route into mortgages, protection and insurance, and tools that help people work out where they stand: a “How can we help?” finder and a mortgage calculator.

Stage 03 · Build

A seamless switch.

Hand-coded and hosted on Cloudflare. Call, WhatsApp and “Speak to an adviser” are one tap away on every page, and an assistant answers questions using the site’s own content.

Everything the business already relied on, from email to booking, kept working through the switch.

Visit quantockfinancial.co.uk

Beyond the website

Referrals in two taps.

Quantock Financial grows by word of mouth, so we built a referral app to make passing on a name effortless. Clients, friends and the team save it to their phone’s home screen like an app, add someone’s details, and the introduction lands straight in the office inbox with the referrer’s name on it.

Each referrer can have a personal link that fills in their name for them, so every introduction is credited to the right person.

We also turned the paper health and lifestyle questionnaire for protection applications into an online form. It adapts for single or joint applications and sends only the answers given, so the advisers have what insurers need first time.

Launch

New website, now live.

A short launch film for Quantock Financial’s social channels, in the same calm palette as the site, walks through the new pages.

Beyond the website · Email signatures

The brand in every inbox.

Most of Quantock Financial’s client conversations happen over email, so the brand needed to carry on into the inbox. We designed an HTML email signature for the whole team in the same deep teal as the site: each adviser’s name, qualifications and direct lines, the office in Taunton, the logo and social links, and the regulatory wording every email has to carry.

Under it, a short list of helpful links puts the referral app, a buildings and contents quote, a free credit report and the Google reviews one click away, so every email quietly does some marketing too.

Each adviser installs theirs from one private page: pick your name, copy, paste into Outlook, Apple Mail or an iPhone. It’s built from tables with hosted images, so it holds together in Outlook and on a phone.
