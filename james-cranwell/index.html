What we do

Our services.

Enquire

Executive Search

We place senior leaders and operations professionals with luxury hotels, restaurants, fashion houses and private members' clubs. Many of the people we introduce are not on the open market.

  • C-suite and general manager appointments
  • Pre-opening and operations leadership
  • Confidential and off-market searches

Strategic Advisory

We advise owners, developers and landlords on positioning and brand partnerships for integrated developments, venues, hotels, resorts and commercial spaces.

  • Concept and positioning
  • Operator and brand partnerships
  • Tenant mix for destination developments

Brand Expansion

We help international brands enter new markets, from restaurants and hotels to spas, retail and fashion. We cover site sourcing and feasibility through to deal negotiation and the relationship after signing.

  • Market entry and site sourcing
  • Feasibility and deal negotiation
  • Post-signing relationship management
