Brand and web studio, Somerset

Branding and websites, made in Somerset.

On The Ground Productions is a brand and web studio based in Somerset, near Taunton. We work out what makes a business different, build the brand around it, then design and hand-code the website. Our clients range from a Taunton mortgage adviser to a motorsport agency in Monaco.

Somerset clients

Work close to home.

Quantock Financial is a mortgage and protection adviser in Taunton. We positioned the business around care rather than products, then designed and built a calm, fast website with a mortgage calculator, an online questionnaire and a launch film.

Quantock Lettings is a family-run letting agent in the Vale of Taunton. It got its own positioning, a website for tenants and landlords, landlord and tenant guides, and pages for seven new homes at Pollards Yard in Bridgwater.

Being local means we can meet you, photograph your team and film your premises ourselves. We take on work across the county, from Taunton and Bridgwater to Yeovil, Wells, Frome and Bath, and over the border in Devon and Bristol.

FAQ

Questions

Are you a web design and branding agency in Somerset?

Yes. On The Ground Productions is a brand and web studio based in Somerset, near Taunton. We design and build brands and websites for businesses in Somerset, across the UK and abroad.

Which parts of Somerset do you cover?

All of it, from Minehead to Frome and Yeovil to Bath, including Keynsham, Midsomer Norton, Bradford on Avon and Trowbridge, plus Devon and Bristol. We’re near Taunton and happy to travel to meet you.

Do you only work with Somerset businesses?

No. We’re based in Somerset and work internationally, with clients in Monaco and across the UK. Most of the work happens on calls and shared links.

How much does a website cost?

Every site is a custom build, so we quote after a short call about what you need. The price depends on the number of pages, the content and features such as booking, calculators or enquiry forms. You’ll get a clear quote before any work starts.

Do you use WordPress, Squarespace or Wix?

No. Every site is designed from scratch and hand-coded, with no templates or page builders. That keeps it fast and secure, and means everything on the page is there for a reason.

Will my website show up on Google and AI search?

SEO and AI search are built in from day one: fast pages, clean code, a sitemap and structured data that tells Google, ChatGPT and other AI assistants who you are and what you do. Where you rank also depends on your content and reputation, and we’ll advise on both.

How do we get started?

Book a 30-minute call or send an enquiry. Tell us what you’re building, and we’ll come back with ideas and a proposal.
