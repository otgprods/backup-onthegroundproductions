Approach

We go and see for ourselves.

OTG started with a camera. Before websites and brands, Marcus Vallance was a documentary photographer, working in refugee camps and communities around the world. That’s still how we make films: go to where the story is, listen, and film what’s real.

For World Mobile we filmed two documentaries on location in Tanzania, made for a global audience deciding whether to believe in the vision. For Fate Sports Group we made the launch reel for their new website.

FAQ

Questions

What kind of films do you make?

Brand films, interviews, documentary pieces, explainers, motion graphics and short content for social and launches. See the full range on our films page.

Do you film outside the UK?

Yes. We go where the story is, including two documentary films on location in Tanzania for World Mobile.

Can you make films for our website?

Yes, and it’s one of the best uses of film. Because we design websites too, the film is made to fit the site: hero loops, case study clips and launch reels.

Do you use AI in video production?

Where it helps, yes, and we’re open about it.
