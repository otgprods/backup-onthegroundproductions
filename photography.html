FAQ

Questions

What kind of photography do you shoot?

Portraits, fashion and beauty, music, travel and lifestyle, plus events, products and brand imagery for campaigns and websites.

Do you photograph live music?

Yes. Matt Cardle, Intimate & Live is a photo booklet we shot of Matt Cardle performing live, with a documentary eye.

Can you shoot photography for our website?

Yes. Because we design and build websites too, we shoot with the layout in mind, so images fit the page rather than being cropped to make them work.

Do you travel for shoots?

Yes. We’re based in Somerset and work internationally, and much of the travel work in this portfolio was shot abroad.

Need photography for your brand?

Portraits, events, products or campaigns, shot with creativity and technical expertise.
