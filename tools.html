Why we share them

Good websites shouldn’t be invisible.

Search is changing. People still use Google, but more and more of them now ask ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity or Copilot who to hire, and those assistants only recommend websites they can read, understand and trust. Plenty of well-designed sites fail that test without anyone noticing, because the problems sit under the surface: a crawler blocked in robots.txt, words that only appear once JavaScript runs, business facts that are never spelled out for machines, or a page that crawls on a phone.

Every website OTG designs and builds goes through these checks before it launches and again after. We use them on our own site as well. Rather than keep them to ourselves, we’ve turned them into tools anyone can run in a minute, on their own site or a competitor’s, with results written in plain English rather than jargon.

Each tool tells you what is working, what to fix first and why it matters. If you’d rather hand the list to someone else, that’s what we do: brand, design and hand-coded websites with SEO and AI search built in from day one, for clients in the UK and internationally.
