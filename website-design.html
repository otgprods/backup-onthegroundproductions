FAQ

Questions

How much does a website cost?

Every site is a custom build, so we quote after a short call about what you need. The price depends on the number of pages, the content and features such as booking, calculators or enquiry forms. You’ll get a clear quote before any work starts.

How long does a website take?

It depends on the size of the site and how much strategy and content it needs. We work in clear stages, strategy, design, build and launch, and you’ll get a timeline with your quote.

Do you use WordPress, Squarespace or Wix?

No. Every site is designed from scratch and hand-coded, with no templates or page builders. That keeps it fast and secure, and means everything on the page is there for a reason.

Will my website show up on Google and AI search?

SEO and AI search are built in from day one: fast pages, clean code, a sitemap and structured data that tells Google, ChatGPT and other AI assistants who you are and what you do. Where you rank also depends on your content and reputation, and we’ll advise on both.

Can you redesign our existing website?

Yes. We can rebuild your current site in a new design and move your content across, keeping your email, booking and other tools working through the switch, as we did for Quantock Financial.

What happens after launch?

We stay on with a monthly care plan for changes, fixes and new content, so the site keeps up with the business. Plans start at £95 a month.

Do you work with clients outside the UK?

Yes. We work with clients across the UK and abroad, including a motorsport management agency in Monaco. Most of the work happens on calls and shared links.
