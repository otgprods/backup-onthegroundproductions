How it looks in dark mode

Best,

Dark-mode mail apps lighten the text automatically, as shown here. The logo tile and the strip stay exactly the same.

Gmail (Google Workspace)

  1. Click Copy signature above.
  2. In Gmail, click the cog (top right), then See all settings.
  3. On the General tab, scroll to Signature. Select the old "OTG" signature and delete it, or click Create new.
  4. Click into the signature box and paste (Cmd + V).
  5. Under Signature defaults, choose it for new emails and replies, then Save Changes at the bottom.

Apple Mail (Mac)

  1. Open this page in Safari and click Copy signature.
  2. In Mail, go to Mail → Settings → Signatures.
  3. Pick your account, click + and name it "OTG".
  4. Untick Always match my default message font.
  5. Delete the placeholder text in the right-hand box and paste (Cmd + V).
  6. Choose it under Choose Signature for that account.

Gmail strips custom fonts from signatures, so the text uses Helvetica and Arial, with a monospace role line to echo the site. On iPhone, set the signature once in Gmail; the Gmail app uses the same one.
