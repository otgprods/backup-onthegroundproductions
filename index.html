It started on the ground.

OTG is led by Marcus Vallance. Before websites and brands, Marcus was a documentary photographer. He started in the refugee camps of Greece and went on to work in camps and communities around the world, with images published worldwide and exhibited internationally.

Alongside the photography, Marcus has produced educational projects and worked on partnerships with organisations including Seiko Epson, the Fulham FC Foundation and the Richard Whitehead Foundation.

“Every brand has a stronger version of itself. Our job is to find it, then help it grow.”

Marcus Vallance, Founder

FAQ

Questions we get asked

What does OTG do?

OTG is a brand and web studio. We build the whole brand: strategy, positioning and identity, custom websites, and film and photography made in-house.

Where are you based?

We’re based in Somerset, near Taunton, and work with clients across the UK and internationally. Calls and most meetings happen online, and we travel for filming and photography. More on our branding and websites for Somerset businesses.

Who do you work with?

Businesses that want to stand out, from a motorsport management agency, a cultural IP studio and a TV garden designer to a mortgage adviser and a lettings agency. See the work.

How much does a website cost?

Every site is a custom build, so we quote after a short call about what you need. The price depends on the number of pages, the content and features such as booking, calculators or enquiry forms. You’ll get a clear quote before any work starts.

Do you use WordPress, Squarespace or Wix?

No. Every site is designed from scratch and hand-coded, with no templates or page builders. That keeps it fast and secure, and means everything on the page is there for a reason.

Will my website show up on Google and AI search?

SEO and AI search are built in from day one: fast pages, clean code, a sitemap and structured data that tells Google, ChatGPT and other AI assistants who you are and what you do. Where you rank also depends on your content and reputation, and we’ll advise on both.

How do we get started?

Book a 30-minute call or send an enquiry. Tell us what you’re building, and we’ll come back with ideas and a proposal.
