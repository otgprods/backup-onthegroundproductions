FAQ

Questions

What is a website care plan?

A monthly plan where we host your site, check it every 5 minutes, keep a daily backup on a separate network, check your email security and make your changes for you. Each month you get a short report on how the site is doing.

What happens if Cloudflare goes down?

Your site runs on Cloudflare, one of the largest networks in the world. We also keep a full copy of it on a separate network, updated every day and every time we make a change. If Cloudflare’s hosting fails, we switch your site to that copy within minutes, and back again once Cloudflare recovers. Online forms and other live features pause until then. In the rare case that Cloudflare’s whole network goes down, including the part that points your domain at your site, the move takes longer: from a few hours to a day or two to reach everyone.

Which plan should I choose?

Essentials suits sites that rarely change. Care suits most businesses whose site changes most months. Growth is for businesses that want the site to bring in more enquiries. If you’re not sure, ask and we’ll tell you honestly.

What happens if I need more time in a month?

Extra time is £85 an hour + VAT, and we always agree it with you before starting. Unused time doesn’t carry over.

Is there a contract?

Three months to start, then month to month. You can cancel with one month’s notice, and your price is fixed for the first 12 months.

Do I own my website?

Yes. You own your site, content and domain. If you leave, we hand everything over and help you move.

Can I have a care plan for a site OTG didn't build?

Usually yes, once we’ve looked at how it’s built. Ask and we’ll check it first.

Not sure which plan fits?

Ask and we’ll tell you honestly. Most clients start on Care and move up or down as the business changes.
