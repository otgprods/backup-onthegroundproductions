SEO and AI Search, Built In From Day One

Search starts with the plan, before the build. Here’s how we shape a site around how people will find the business.

By Marcus Vallance, Founder, On The Ground Productions · 3 min read · 2 November 2026

In short

  • Search is planned before the build, so the site’s structure matches how people look for the business.
  • Structured data, clear writing and permanent redirects help Google and AI assistants understand the site.
  • A Google Business Profile and real reviews matter too. Nobody can honestly promise a top ranking.

A lot of websites get built first and optimised for search afterwards. We plan for search before the build starts.

People find businesses in more ways than they used to. They search on Google. They ask an AI assistant. They look at a map. A site needs to make sense to all of them, and that starts with the plan.

Start with how people will search

Before the build, we ask how people will look for the business. Then we shape the site around the answer.

Take a sports management agency. People search for athletes by name. So every athlete it represents gets a page of their own, and a search for an athlete leads straight to the agency.

Say clearly what the business is

Search engines and assistants both need to know what a business is, who it’s for and where it works. Some of that is the words on the page. Some of it is structured data, a layer behind each page that tells Google who you are and what you do, in a form it can read directly. We build it in from the start.

Never break a link

When a new site replaces an old one, addresses change. Every address Google already knows should still lead somewhere useful, so we set up permanent redirects and nothing turns into a dead end.

Written for AI search too

More and more people ask an assistant instead of typing a search. Assistants answer from what they can read and understand, so clear, well-structured writing helps.

That same content can work on the site itself. An assistant can answer visitors’ questions using the site’s own content.

The parts outside the website

Some of the biggest signals aren’t on the site at all. For a local business, a Google Business Profile and real reviews matter a lot. Showing your Google rating at the top of the homepage means the reviews work twice.

What we won’t promise

Nobody can honestly promise a place at the top of Google, so we won’t. What we can do is build a site that gives a business the best chance: clear, fast, well structured and easy to understand.
